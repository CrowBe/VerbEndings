# Researcher Sheet Brief (batch 1)

## Canon And Role

The Researcher is the facility human who observes Trials from the Assignment Deck / booth. Capable and curious, physically fallible, never heroic or militarised. Only the Researcher may call a Subject a blob.

## Production Sheet Goals

- Front, side, back, and three-quarter full-body turnaround.
- Face and hair close-ups; practical clothing and hand detail.
- Scale view beside a **mouthless** neutral Shared Subject (~1.2 m Subject vs taller Researcher).
- Booth-operating pose and at least one clumsy physical-comedy key.

## Exclusions

No measurement rulers or height charts, no captions, no Material Dispenser silhouette, no holographic UI, no militarised gear. Subject in scale panels must stay mouthless with neutral cream substrate and short white jacket (no permanent Verb colour, no SAD SHIELD text).
