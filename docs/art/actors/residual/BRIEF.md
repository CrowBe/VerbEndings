# Residual Sheet Brief (batch 1)

## Canon And Role

A Residual is a worn living Droplet-ancestry Creature in Adaptive Containment and Collapse contexts. It shares soft-substrate ancestry with the Subject but must **not** copy stable Subject identity (no neat lab jacket identity, no suit number).

## Production Sheet Goals

- Front, side, back, and three-quarter turnaround of a worn Residual.
- Disposal garment / wrap evidence (fray, holes, grommet) rather than a pristine Subject suit.
- Softened or asymmetrical topology readable as unstable without body-horror gore.
- Deformation keys (walk / prone) and a side-by-side comparison with a clean mouthless Subject.

## Exclusions

No Subject lab-coat identity, no identity badges or numbers, no captions, no energy beams, no melting puddles as primary read, no zombie or gore treatment.
