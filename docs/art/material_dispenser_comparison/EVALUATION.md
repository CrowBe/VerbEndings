# Material Dispenser Asset Evaluation

## Retained Decision

The retained result is a paired concept-to-proxy set rather than one winner:

- `assets/source/props/material_dispenser/material_dispenser_meshy6_concept.glb` is the high-detail shape reference. It preserves the approved turnaround most closely but is not runtime geometry.
- `assets/source/props/material_dispenser/material_dispenser_blender_mcp.blend` is the editable authored source.
- `assets/exports/props/material_dispenser_blender_mcp.glb` is the cleaned proxy export. It satisfies the scale, origin, topology-budget, naming, material-separation, and collision-boundary contracts.

The comparison export remains evidence for the asset pipeline and is not referenced by gameplay scenes. The live `MaterialDispenser.tscn` references the separate production-preparation export at `assets/exports/props/material_dispenser.glb`.

## Visual Judgement

Meshy 6 produced the strongest high-detail interpretation of the turnaround. The pre-remesh result preserved the shell, panel hierarchy, base, vent, and aperture assembly more closely than the procedural build. Its density and fused structure make it a concept source rather than an editable game prop.

The 5,000-triangle Meshy remesh did not preserve that advantage. It lost panel and aperture definition, produced 162 non-manifold edges after welding, supplied no semantic material or component separation, and returned at 0.12 m height despite automatic sizing.

The Blender MCP result is visibly simpler and needs further art direction around the protective panels, top cap, and integrated nozzle silhouette. It nevertheless retains the relationships required for production iteration: exact dimensions, predictable origin and facing, named parts, discrete materials, authored state seams, and clean topology.

## Runtime Validation

The retained proxy imports and instantiates under Godot 4.3 with 49 mesh instances, 5,364 triangles, five expected materials, 1.6 m width, 2.0 m height, floor offset zero, and no imported collision. The project import and eight-second runtime smoke complete without engine or script errors.

Godot's optional generated-LOD pass emitted a non-finite-normal warning for the multi-object bevel geometry even after degenerate-face cleanup. The retained `.glb.import` disables that pass for this modest 5,364-triangle proxy; the normal import is warning-free. Production LODs are outside this proxy's readiness level and are justified by profiling rather than generated by default.

## Production Interpretation

For hard-surface facility props, Meshy is most useful upstream of the runtime mesh:

1. settle canon, dimensions, and a consistent multi-view reference;
2. generate and retain the high-detail Meshy output as a shape study;
3. use Blender MCP to reconstruct or deliberately retopologise the approved silhouette into named components;
4. normalise scale and origin, author material boundaries, remove degenerate geometry, and export GLB;
5. keep collision and gameplay markers in Godot;
6. verify the actual export in Godot 4.3.

Automatic low-poly remeshing is not treated as production cleanup when it destroys authored relationships or produces invalid topology. The high-detail concept remains valuable even when its automatic runtime derivative is rejected.
