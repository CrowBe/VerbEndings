Creative direction

Source: docs/WORLD_DESIGN.md · Creative Direction

VerbEndings is clinical, buoyant, tactile, overconfident, and quietly uncanny. Friendly physical comedy sits inside an industrial research facility that treats language and living Subjects as measurable inputs.

The Semantic Engine communicates through plans, panels, lights, printers, and machinery. It has no avatar or conversational persona. The Researcher is the sole regular speaking character.

Domain model (participants)

Source: GLOSSARY.md · Participants And Subjects

  • A Player is the human or network participant.
  • A Subject is the living playable entity controlled by a Player during a Trial.
  • Semantic Adaptation Droplet (SAD) is the facility's formal classification for the adaptive substrate from which a Subject originates.
  • Blob is the Researcher's sole affectionate colloquialism for a Subject.
  • A Verb is a single-word capability issued by the Semantic Engine and always written in capitals: GROW, BURN, FREEZE.

Trial lifecycle

Source: GLOSSARY.md · Programme And Trial Lifecycle

The Ultimate Lexical Trial Programme is the ongoing institutional programme. A Trial is one complete game session and contains exactly three Rounds. Each Round contains one scored Experiment.

After Verb Selection, each Round follows: Pair Draft → Experiment Brief → Laboratory Entry → Experiment → Recall → Result Announcement → optional Thematic Moment.

Compound topology

Source: docs/WORLD_DESIGN.md · Compound Topology

The Compound is a faceted hemisphere with a hexagonal plan around a vertical Central Core. Its main floor is organised as three repeating nominal 120-degree territories, each with one wedge-shaped Laboratory. An elevated annular Assignment Deck surrounds the Core.
